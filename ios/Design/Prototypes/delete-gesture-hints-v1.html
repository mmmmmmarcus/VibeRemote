A hint when you need it.

Three ways to reveal “hold, then slide left to clear” without adding noise to everyday typing.

My recommendation: reveal on hold.

Keep the familiar Delete icon at rest. After the 380 ms hold threshold, show a small leftward cue beside the key, away from the thumb. The cue disappears immediately on release. No looping animation, no permanent badge.

The prototypes keep today’s behavior: a tap deletes one character, holding repeats, and a held 60 pt leftward slide clears immediately. The red state confirms that action. During dictation, entering Delete cancels the current recording; the same red feedback keeps its meaning consistent.

Use the demo button or hold Delete and drag left yourself. These are visual explorations, not changes to the installed keyboard. Reduce Motion is respected.
