01 / MAKE ROOM

Letters dissolve together.

A 120 ms fade. No ripple, drifting letters or moving keys. Delete and Return stay legible.

02 / JUST ENOUGH

Light follows the voice.

A quick rise, a soft settle. Only a slight change in brightness. Pauses return the keys to stillness.

03 / STEP ASIDE

Release means quiet.

The letters return. Polishing happens in the text field, with no new animation on the keyboard.
