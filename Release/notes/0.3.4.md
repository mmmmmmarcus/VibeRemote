## A closer look at your remote

- Settings is now one-third narrower, with controls and hover labels fitted to the compact layout.
- The aluminum remote has a lighter satin-silver finish and clearer metallic reflections. Fixed exposure prevents it from gradually becoming overbright after opening Settings.
- Getting Started now places the remote on a sky-blue painted background above short explanations, highlighting each control step by step for your remote generation.
- The walkthrough finishes with Siri shortcut setup. Closing it leaves your saved mapping unchanged.

For Apple Silicon Macs running macOS 27. Existing mappings and auto-disconnect settings are preserved. Downloads and installation remain your choice.

The unchanged GPL audio driver's corresponding source is included. Apple PacketLogger is not redistributed.
